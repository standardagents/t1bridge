# T1Bridge v1 release and validation checklist

Updated 2026-09-06. **The repository and signed packages are public and
announced.** This record separates completed delivery from remaining validation;
an unchecked item is not a claim that publication is still waiting for approval.
Publication and general success reports do not establish untested behavior.

Scope: core, DKMS, matched libfprint/fprintd packages and their public contracts.
Omarchy installer/onboarding and optional companion UIs/providers have separate
acceptance and do not block use of the shipped packages.

## Known unsupported functionality

- **System suspend/resume:** not working on the owner's machine; not supported
  currently. T1 recovery through sleep/wake remains in scope. Display blanking
  and wake are not evidence of system suspend. Do not force an unsafe host
  suspend path just to fill a checklist.
- **Runtime T1 power saving:** autosuspend is disabled for stability.
- **Ambient-light sensor:** stock HID/IIO readings are present on the tested
  machine; desktop discovery is packaged in v0.1.8. Broader validation remains in
  [#17](https://github.com/standardagents/t1bridge/issues/17). See
  [sensor support](../ambient-light.md) for evidence and remaining gaps.
- **General Secure Enclave key services:** not shipped.

Kernel upstream submissions, other-distribution packaging and companion
applications are follow-up work, not reasons to rebuild the current packages.
